01 Overview

One brand. Eight front doors.

The challenge

Eight clinics, one website

Patients search locally: "physical therapy in Katy", "PT near Sugar Land". Each clinic needed to rank and convert on its own, without eight separate websites.

  • Every branch has its own address, phone, email, hours and team.
  • Branches serve different communities and insurance mixes.
  • Conditions (knee, back, shoulder…) are the same everywhere and shouldn't be written 8 times.
  • The clinic team must update content without a developer.
What I designed & built

A tree, not a pile of pages

I designed the site structure first: a hub for each branch, shared condition pages they all link to, and conversion pages every path ends in. Then I designed the UI and built it as a custom WordPress theme.

  • A page template per branch, built from a shared library of section modules.
  • Content in ACF fields, so the clinic edits text, photos and details itself.
  • Each branch combines only the modules it needs, in its own order.
  • Every branch links to every condition page: strong internal linking for local SEO.

05 Key pages

Designed to book visits.

  • Branch contact card up top: address, phone, email, fax and opening hours, plus request-appointment and call buttons.
  • Condition carousel linking to the shared pain-relief pages.
  • Insurance logos and options where a branch needs them, with a "verify your coverage" path.
  • Community section for branches serving the South Asian & Indian community, with Hindi and Gujarati-speaking staff.
  • Facility gallery, rehab methods and a booking form on every branch.
  • Sticky "Book now" button on every page, and a home-page branch finder with live maps.

Designed mobile-first: most patients find a clinic on their phone.

01

Structure before pixels

I mapped the tree first: branch hubs, shared condition pages, conversion pages. The design followed the structure, not the other way round.

02

Templates + ACF

A template per branch on a shared module library, with every text, photo and detail in ACF fields, so the clinic updates content without me.

03

Local SEO by design

Branch-specific H1s and content, Yoast SEO on every page and 48 branch → condition links help each clinic rank in its own area.

04

Built for real patients

Click-to-call, maps, hours and a sticky Book Now button on every page, plus forms protected by honeypot and anti-spam plugins.

05

Motion with purpose

AOS reveals, Swiper carousels for conditions and facilities, and GSAP touches, kept light so pages stay fast on mobile.

06

One brand, many voices

The same visual system flexes per branch: sports rehab, post-surgical care, community focus and insurance options, each where it belongs.
