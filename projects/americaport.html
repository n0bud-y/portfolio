01 Overview

One checkout. Many sellers.

The challenge

A marketplace, not just a store

The client wanted a home for American-made products where many independent sellers list in one place. That means more than a shop theme:

  • Buyers need to trust that "Made in USA" really means it.
  • Customers pay once, even when the cart holds items from several sellers.
  • The platform keeps its commission and pays every seller accurately.
  • Each seller should only see and manage their own products and orders.
  • The team edits the homepage and store details without a developer.
The solution

Two apps, one Laravel core

I built a fast, SEO-friendly storefront rendered by Laravel, and a separate admin console as a Vue 2 + Vuetify single-page app talking to the same Laravel backend through a JSON API.

  • 3-tier verification badge on every product, shown as a colored star.
  • Stripe card checkout with state-based tax and a clear 3-step flow.
  • Orders split per vendor; an order completes only when every vendor has fulfilled.
  • Per-item commission ledger and manual payouts with full history.
  • Role & permission system reused for vendors, plus a no-code homepage builder.

02 How it works

The complete flow, end to end.

Follow one product from a seller's dashboard to a customer's door, and the money back to the seller.

  2. 1
    Vendor

    A seller lists a product

    Vendors log in to the same console with a limited role. They add the product, choose its "Made in USA" tier, pick categories and generate size / color variations, each with its own price, image and stock.

  3. 2
    Admin

    The catalog goes live

    Admins publish or feature products, organise the category tree and control which collections appear on the homepage. The storefront updates the moment they save.

  4. 3
    Customer

    Shoppers find & trust it

    Search with a category dropdown, filter by price, category, size, stock and sale, then pick a variation on the product page. The verification star tells them exactly how American-made it is.

  5. 4
    Checkout

    One payment with Stripe

    A 3-step Cart → Checkout → Confirmation flow. Tax is calculated from the shipping state, shipping is free because sellers ship directly, and the card is charged securely by Stripe.

  6. 5
    Fulfilment

    Each vendor ships their part

    The order is split by vendor. Every vendor marks their items complete; the admin sees "Waiting for vendors: 0 of 1" and can only close the order once every vendor has fulfilled. Orders can be downloaded for records.

  7. 6
    Finance

    Commission & payouts

    Every sold item lands in the vendor's ledger with sale, commission and "vendor gets". Admins pay selected items or everything unpaid, record the transfer, and the payout history shows exactly which items it covered.

Where the money goes

Customers pay the platform once. The platform keeps its commission and pays each vendor what they earned.

04 Admin console

13 modules. One console.

A Vue 2 + Vuetify single-page app on top of the Laravel API. Pick a module:

Know the store at a glance

  • KPI cards: total sales, total, paid and delivered orders.
  • 30-day revenue chart and orders per month for the last 12 months.
  • Revenue by period (week, month, 3 months, year), paid orders only.
  • Quick actions and a "store at a glance" summary of catalog and audience.

Orders that understand vendors

  • Filter by text, order status, payment status and date range.
  • "Vendor progress" column, e.g. 0/3 completed, for multi-seller orders.
  • Order detail with customer & shipping info, tax, Stripe payment status and per-item vendor status.
  • Status changes are gated until every vendor has fulfilled; orders can be downloaded.

A real ledger, not a spreadsheet

  • Money flow summary: customers paid, admin commission, paid to vendors, still owed.
  • Per-vendor table with commission rate, sales, admin cut, paid out, in payout and owed.
  • Vendor page with an item-level ledger, payout details (bank / PayPal) and "pay selected" / "pay all unpaid".
  • Payout history with Pending / Paid / Cancelled tabs; expand a payout to see the exact items it covers.

A product builder with variations

  • Normal or variational products, assigned to a vendor or kept as the store's own.
  • Labels (size, color…) → "Generate variations" builds every combination, each with price, image, stock and a default.
  • "Made by" tier, discount %, similar products, category tree and a rich-text (CKEditor) description.
  • Publish / feature switches; categories with parent & sub-categories; reusable labels.

Everyone sees only what they should

  • Users split into customers, vendors and administrators.
  • 55 granular permissions (view / create / edit / delete per module) grouped into roles.
  • The Vendor role gets 8 of them: their own products, orders, labels, finance and payouts.
  • Engagement tools: contact inquiries, newsletter subscribers with CSV export, and review moderation.

Edit the homepage without code

  • Homepage sections in order, each with a show / hide switch.
  • Banner image upload, headings, highlighted title, paragraph and button link.
  • Review widget: best-rated automatically or hand-picked, how many and minimum rating.
  • Homepage navigation (category / product links with images) and store contact & social links.

06 Highlights

What I'm proud of.

01

Item-level money tracking

Commission is recorded per order item, so every payout traces back to the exact products it pays for: no guesswork when sellers ask "what is this for?".

02

Multi-vendor order logic

One customer order, many sellers. Vendor progress is tracked per item and the order can't be closed until every vendor has fulfilled their part.

03

One permission system for all

Instead of a separate vendor portal, vendors use the same console with a scoped role: 8 of 55 permissions, only their own data.

04

Variation generator

Pick label values like sizes and colors and the console builds every combination with its own price, image and stock.

05

No-code homepage

Sections, banner, navigation, reviews widget and store details are all editable and go live on save: the client never needs a developer for content.

06

Trust built into the UI

The 3-tier "Made in USA" system runs from the product editor to colored stars on every card, so the brand promise is visible everywhere.
